# Self-Hosting

Atomic is available today as a hosted service through
[Atomic Storage](https://atomic.storage/?utm_source=docs.atomic.dev&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=self-hosting), and that is the recommended way to run
Atomic for your team: we handle the storage, availability, and upgrades while
you focus on your code and your agents.

We **do not currently offer a self-hosting package** for the Atomic server,
except to our [founding teams](https://atomic.dev/founding/?utm_source=docs.atomic.dev&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=self-hosting).

## Founding teams

Founding teams get early access to — and a voice in — the Atomic platform,
including deployment options that are not yet generally available. If running
Atomic inside your own infrastructure matters to your team, this is the place to
start.

[Join as a founding team](https://atomic.dev/founding/?utm_source=docs.atomic.dev&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=self-hosting) and tell us what you
need. We work directly with founding teams to shape the platform around their
real requirements, so the more you tell us about your deployment constraints —
compliance, network isolation, existing infrastructure — the better.

## Explore options

Want to know whether a self-hosted or dedicated deployment fits your team?
Sign up at [atomic.dev/founding](https://atomic.dev/founding/?utm_source=docs.atomic.dev&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=self-hosting) and we'll
follow up to explore the options with you.
